Sample 1: AI answer evaluation

Question: “How does photosynthesis work in plants?”

Answer A

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to make their own food. They take in carbon dioxide from the air and absorb water through their roots. Using energy from sunlight, captured by the green pigment chlorophyll, plants convert these materials into glucose, a type of sugar they use for energy and growth. Oxygen is produced during the process and released into the air.

Answer B

Photosynthesis is basically how plants use sunlight to make food. They take in sunlight and water from the soil, then make air in their leaves. The green parts of the plant help with the process, and the plant eventually releases bubbles.

My ratings

Answer A: 9/10 This answer is clear, accurate and easy to follow. It names the key ingredients (sunlight, carbon dioxide and water) and the key results (glucose and oxygen). It brings in chlorophyll without piling on detail, so the reader gets a solid picture of the process in a few sentences. I took one point off because it never says the process happens mainly in the leaves.

Answer B: 3/10 This one gets the basic idea that sunlight and leaves are involved, but it is too vague and has several mistakes. Saying plants “make air” is so simplified that it misleads, and carbon dioxide is never mentioned. “Bubbles” does not explain how oxygen is released. A simple answer is fine, but it still needs to be accurate.
